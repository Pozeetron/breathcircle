Privacy Policy for Breath Circle

Last updated: October 11, 2026
Developer: Evol
Package: com.evol.breathcircle

Breath Circle (“the App”) is a local breathing-practice tool. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not process.

1. Summary

Breath Circle is designed to work on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a user-profile backend for this App. The App does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or sale.

2. Information the App uses

  • Device language. The App reads the system language to show the interface in a supported language (falling back to English).
  • Local preferences. Session choices such as selected breathing technique, cycle count, and haptic options may be kept temporarily in memory while you use the App. They are not uploaded by the App to our servers.
  • Haptics / vibration. If enabled, the App uses the device vibrator for breath-phase feedback. This happens entirely on-device.

3. Information we do not collect

Breath Circle does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. The App does not include social login and does not sell personal data.

4. Permissions

  • Vibrate — to provide optional haptic cues for inhale / hold / exhale phases.
  • Wake lock — to keep the screen awake during an active breathing session.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Health disclaimer

Breath Circle provides general wellness timing guidance only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

7. Third-party services

The current Android release is intended to operate without analytics SDKs that identify users. If this changes in a future version, this Policy will be updated before the change is published.

8. Data retention and security

Because Breath Circle does not maintain a cloud account for user content, there is no server-side retention of breathing-session history for this App. Keep your device protected with a screen lock and OS updates.

9. Your choices

You can disable vibration in the App, force-stop or uninstall the App at any time, and control app permissions in Android system settings.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Breath Circle, contact: privacy@evol.app (replace with your public developer support email before publishing).
